A few things about the numbers.

How are days and milestones counted?

Days lived is the number of calendar days between your birthday and the selected date. On your birthday, it is 0. In the default mode, the 100-day milestone is your birthday plus 100 days. Choose “Birth = day 1” to find your literal 100th day instead, which is your birthday plus 99 days. The days-lived total always shows elapsed days.

What about leap years, time zones, and exact birth times?

Leap years are included. “Today” uses your device’s local date. Calendar arithmetic is independent of daylight saving changes. This calculator does not ask for a birth time, so hours, minutes, and seconds are equivalents of calendar days, not exact elapsed time. A February 29 birthday uses February 28 as its anniversary in non-leap years. Month calculations clamp to the last valid day of a month.

Does this website save my birthday?

Calculations happen on your device. There are no analytics, external fonts, or accounts. Your birthday is saved in this browser only if you select “Remember my birthday.” Use “Clear my dates” to remove it. Calendar and CSV downloads contain your milestone dates, so share them thoughtfully.

Can I look at a date in the past or future?

Yes. Change “Calculate up to” to explore any date on or after your birthday, through year 9998. The next birthday, countdowns, and milestone statuses all use that date. Future birth dates are allowed for planning. This is a calendar tool, not a prediction of lifespan.

* Time equivalents use 24 hours per calendar day, 60 minutes per hour, and 60 seconds per minute.
